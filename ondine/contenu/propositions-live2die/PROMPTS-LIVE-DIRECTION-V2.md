# Direction photographique V2

## Mode

Génération d’images intégrée. Les images de Live2die ont servi uniquement à analyser la grammaire générale du vêtement ; elles n’ont pas été fournies au générateur et aucun de leurs motifs n’a été copié.

## Grammaire retenue

- scènes humaines photographiques transformées en pochoirs xerox ;
- sujets anonymes, sans célébrité ni personnage protégé ;
- narration de fuite, d’attente, d’isolement et de distance ;
- scènes dispersées et répétitions imparfaites ;
- très grands espaces non imprimés ;
- peinture bordeaux physique suivant le coton et les coutures ;
- absence de pictogrammes propres, de cercles, de barres et d’emblèmes centrés.

## Prompt maître — prototypes produit

Use case: product-mockup.

Asset type: original one-of-one underground streetwear concept for a collection review.

Primary request: create a premium straight-on product photograph of an oversized heavyweight garment transformed into a raw hand-painted one-of-one piece.

Scene/backdrop: isolated garment on a pure black studio background, entire garment visible, no human model or hanger.

Garment graphic: three or four ORIGINAL anonymous human scenes printed directly on the fabric as harsh black or white photographic stencils with rough photocopy edges. Use a woman running away, a seated adult deeply hunched on a plain chair with empty hands, a solitary standing figure and a tiny person walking out of frame. Keep the scenes disconnected and preserve large areas of untouched fabric.

Hand paint: dried dark burgundy pigment physically splattered and dragged across selected areas of the garment. Paint must interact with seams, cotton grain and folds as real hand paint.

Style/medium: raw DIY underground streetwear, crude hand screen-print, distressed xerox photography, imperfect registration, cracked ink, one-of-one finish, bleak narrative.

Constraints: completely original anonymous subjects; no text, logo or brand mark; no circles, halos, decorative bars, roses, birds, skulls or religious symbols; no weapon, injury, explicit violence or self-harm action; no celebrity, copyrighted character or recreation of an existing product; avoid vector icons, pictograms and polished symmetry.

## Prompt maître — scènes transparentes

Use case: stylized-concept.

Asset type: source sheet of original photographic stencil figures for screen-print compositing.

Primary request: create a precise 2-by-2 contact sheet containing four completely separate original anonymous human figure cutouts: a woman running away in a long loose dress, an adult seated deeply hunched on a plain chair, a solitary standing adult with head bowed, and an adult walking away. A second sheet contains a crouched adult covering the face, a sprinting adult, a kneeling woman reaching toward empty ground and a person seated alone on a bench.

Visual treatment: harsh pure-black photographic xerox stencils, real anatomy and clothing folds, damaged photocopy grain, irregular eroded edges and sparse ink loss.

Background: genuine transparent alpha.

Constraints: no text, borders, logos, color, weapons, injuries, self-harm actions, celebrities or copyrighted characters; avoid stick figures, cartoons, vector icons and overlapping subjects.

## Livrables issus de ces prompts

- 4 prototypes premium générés directement ;
- 8 scènes photographiques originales sur fond transparent ;
- 100 mockups V2 composés à partir de ces scènes ;
- 1 planche récapitulative numérotée.
