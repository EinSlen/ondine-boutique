Production decision

Eight suppliers.
Seven product families.

La collection couvre désormais jersey, tee, hoodies, knit, denim, trackwear et accessoires. Un seul fabricant ne sera pas le meilleur sur tout : il faut regrouper le jersey chez un atelier, puis spécialiser denim, maille, cagoule et bijou.

Route recommandée

Prototyper les vêtements jersey chez ASBX ou White Cotton. Traiter le denim séparément. Tester l'écharpe chez Wildemasche, la cagoule chez Humboo et les boucles chez Argentum avant tout bulk.

04 / Email supplier

Send one clear brief.

Subject: SOUR HOURS Drop 01 — samples and production quote

Hello,

We are developing a twelve-piece European streetwear capsule. Please quote only the product families your factory produces in-house or through disclosed partners.

The range includes a racing jersey, heavyweight long sleeve, wide track pants, baggy washed denim, hoodies, a T-shirt, jacquard knitwear, a scarf, a balaclava and earrings. Please quote sample cost and available quantity tiers per style, including labels, packaging and shipping to France.

Please confirm fabric composition, actual GSM, measurement chart, shrinkage tolerance, wash options, decoration limitations and lead times. We need digital artwork approval and a physical sample before bulk production.

Attached: product mockups, SVG assets and placement sheet.

Thank you.
Ne pas publier les compositions, grammages, délais ou disponibilités tant que le fournisseur ne les a pas confirmés et que le sample n'a pas passé le test de lavage.
