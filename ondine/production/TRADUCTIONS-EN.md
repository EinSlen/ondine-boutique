## PAGES

## a-propos — About
D2M is a streetwear label built on simple images: short scenes, black and off-white, one colour of red. NOTHING LASTS is the first release. Twelve pieces, each one a flat composition. On the zip hoodies the zip runs through the artwork rather than around it. Everything is printed to order in Europe. Nothing is warehoused, nothing is restocked. When a release closes, it closes. Designed in France.

## contact — Contact
A question about an order, a size, or a release? Use the form below and we will get back to you.

## guide-des-tailles — Size guide
Tops run S to XXL. Bottoms run 2XS to 3XL. Every piece is cut oversized: dropped shoulders on the tees and hoodies, a wide straight leg on the bottoms. If you want a closer fit, take your usual size. If you want the fit in the photos, take one up. The full measurement table is published once the production blank is locked. We would rather leave it out than print numbers we cannot stand behind.

## livraison — Shipping
Every piece is printed to order, so the total time is production plus transit. The exact options and costs for your address are shown at checkout. France: standard 7,99 €, free from 65 €. Express 10,99 €. European Union: standard 22 €. Rest of the world: standard 29 €. Tracking is sent by email as soon as the parcel leaves the production facility.

## retours — Returns
Right of withdrawal. You have 14 days from delivery to withdraw from your order, subject to the exceptions set out by law. Contact us through the contact page with your order number. The piece must come back in a resalable condition: tried on, not worn out, not washed. Return shipping is at your expense unless the piece arrived damaged or was not the one you ordered. Once the return is received and checked, the refund is issued to the original payment method within 14 days. Damaged or wrong item. Send us a photo within 14 days of delivery and we replace it or refund it in full, return shipping included.

Tops S to XXL, bottoms 2XS to 3XL, cut oversized. The measurement table is added to the size guide once the production blank is locked. Where is it made?
