# Capsule éditoriale v2 — concepts

## Direction

Trois propositions originales pour dead2me : zip noir autour de la séparation, tee-shirt écru autour de l'absence, hoodie noir autour de l'effacement. Palette charbon, écru et bordeaux ; petits récits visuels et textures de trame plutôt qu'une multiplication de logos.

La planche est un concept généré avec l'outil intégré de génération d'images. Les coupes, placements et techniques devront être adaptés au vêtement fournisseur réellement choisi. Ne pas utiliser cette planche comme preuve de fabrication, photo d'échantillon ou maquette finale pour le fournisseur.

## Prompt utilisé

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: one coherent clothing design presentation board for original independent brand dead2me. Primary request: three premium melancholic underground streetwear garment concepts, dark raw understated narrative graphics, original artwork not copying any existing brand. Wide landscape triptych, equal three columns on very light warm grey background, no header, no labels, no typography outside the garments. Photorealistic straight-on flat lay photographic mockups, excellent true fabric texture, deep blacks, neutral daylight, precise seams, three separate garments fully visible with equal generous margin. LEFT: plain black regular relaxed zip hoodie, full zipper, kangaroo pockets, no artificial acid washing, small rough ivory halftone print showing two anonymous figures standing far apart on either side of the zipper, one thin faded burgundy interrupted line between them, tiny lowercase dead2me at bottom of print. CENTER: natural off-white box tee, a small weathered charcoal photographic print of an empty metal chair beside a doorway, miniature burgundy words 'still waiting' beneath, ample negative space. RIGHT: back view of black relaxed pullover hoodie, a medium sized weathered ivory photographic halftone of a lone human silhouette fading like a long exposure with a small dark burgundy imperfect circle near the shoulder, very small 'nothing lasts' below. Graphics are tactile sparse imperfect screenprint-like, not cartoon stick figures, not vector clip art, not giant logos. Avoid visible weapons, blood, graphic harm, extra objects, labels, ribbons, border decorations, watermarks. Keep all concepts realistically producible as prints on conventional blanks, no embroidery, no distressing holes, no fantasy seams.
