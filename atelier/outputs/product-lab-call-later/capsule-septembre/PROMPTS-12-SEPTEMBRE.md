Clever typography that looks authored, not a generic system bold font, not ornate gothic and not bubbly cartoon. Both words fully legible. Solid deep black ink only. No icon, animal, star, globe, halo, ribbon, subtitle, date or slogan.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha, clean edges, no white rectangle, no simulated checkerboard.
Materials: FLAT black ink artwork, no textile, no garment, no photo, no shadow, no raised/puff print or 3D. Generous clear transparent margin all around. No competitor name, trademark or replica of an existing brand wordmark.
Output: one square raster print-art exploration at maximum practical native resolution.
