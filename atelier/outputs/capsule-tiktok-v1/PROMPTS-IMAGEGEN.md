# Prompts ImageGen — mode intégré

La génération d’images a servi uniquement à produire les bases raster difficiles à obtenir proprement en SVG : deux vêtements vierges et deux silhouettes originales. Les compositions, textes et fichiers d’impression ont ensuite été construits localement.

## Blank bone long-sleeve

Use case: product-mockup. Blank apparel mockup for compositing an original fashion graphic. Photorealistic overhead flat-lay product photograph of one completely blank off-white heavyweight long-sleeve T-shirt, front side facing camera, sleeves fully extended and entirely visible, centered for precise artwork placement. Pure matte black seamless background, no props. Premium streetwear catalog photography, natural cotton texture, subtle washed vintage finish, realistic collar, seams, folds and shadows. Square image, full garment visible with generous black margin on every side, straight-on top-down camera. Soft neutral studio light. Garment absolutely blank; no print, splatter, distress holes, label text, logo or watermark.

## Collapsed performer silhouette

Use case: logo-brand. Transparent apparel-print silhouette. One original full-body solid-black silhouette of a fictional long-haired alternative-rock performer collapsing backward onto one knee, torso arched slightly, one hand shielding the side of the face and the other arm hanging down. Dramatic and emotionally exhausted pose with a clean readable outline. Single flat ink silhouette with rough analog screen-print edge; genuinely transparent background. No weapon, firearm, injury, wound, blood, text, logo or watermark; not based on a real person.
